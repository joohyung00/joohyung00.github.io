From LILaC and FiF to Real-World Multimodal Retrieval

What my two papers solved, what a practical web-scale multimodal retriever still needs (raw HTML, reactive pages, more modalities, grounding, budgets), how recent work approaches each gap, and four research directions.

Paper readingDSLab NLP seminar · POSTECH · 2024.12.11

MuRAG: Multimodal Retrieval-Augmented Generator for Open Question Answering over Images and Text

Chen et al., EMNLP 2022 · opens with ACORN (Patel et al., SIGMOD 2024)

Multimodal RAG with one universal encoder and decoder: the two challenges (handling mixed modalities, training them), in-batch pretraining, two-stage fine-tuning, and the error analysis. Opens with a six-slide recap of ACORN, predicate-agnostic hybrid vector search.

GRAG: Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Hu et al., arXiv 2024

RAG over textual graphs: retrieving a query-relevant subgraph with a soft pruner, feeding the subgraph's text and structure to the LLM, and results on WebQSP and ExplaGraphs.

Paper readingDSLab NLP seminar · POSTECH · 2024.10.07

Improving Language Models by Retrieving from Trillions of Tokens (RETRO)

Borgeaud et al., ICML 2022 · opens with DiskANN (Subramanya et al., NeurIPS 2019)

Token-level RAG built up on one diagram: why per-token retrieval is slow, then chunked retrieval, chunked cross-attention, and frozen BERT embedders, each introduced as the fix to a specific problem. Opens with a seven-slide recap of DiskANN.

GRAM: Generative Retrieval Augmented Matching of Data Schemas in the Context of Data Security

LLM-based schema matching for data ingestion when the data itself cannot be shown: object-type detection, reducing the task to attribute mapping, NER and double-RAG filters, the key detector, and the ablations. Ends with my own take.
