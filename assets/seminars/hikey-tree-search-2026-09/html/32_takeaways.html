Takeaways

The hierarchy is a free signal

Documents already carry the structure that should steer search. This is why the lineage moved from chunk similarity to hierarchy traversal.

Narrow, then bundle

HiKEY's answer: narrow in two stages, documents → sections (zero LLM calls), then fill the budget core-first. A combination tuned to the industrial setting: thousands of docs, tables and figures, a finite reader budget.

The open question (discussion)

Whichever direction the traversal takes, and however it is trained, it happens over headings and summaries. A fact they fail to carry is invisible to every method here. Who breaks this limit?

One line · document-QA search has become a hierarchy-traversal problem, and HiKEY is where that traversal converges for multimodal industrial documents: cheap and robust
